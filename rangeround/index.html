Range Round

Play a round of golf from the driving range.

Contact

Questions, bug reports or ideas: email kb4902713.apps@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version if something isn't working.

How it works

  1. Tap New round, choose a course and difficulty, and tap Tee off.
  2. Hit your shot at the range. Tap Enter shot, pick your club, and tap the range picture where the ball landed. Drag or use the arrows to adjust, then tap Hit.
  3. The app moves your ball on the hole and gives you yardages for the next shot. Once you're on the green, putts are added for you.

Common questions

Does the distance I enter include roll? Yes. Enter where the ball finished, the way a range's yardage signs measure it.

My range uses limited-flight balls. Turn on Range ball adjustment in Rules & clubs to add distance back to each shot.

How are putts counted? By default, 1 putt inside 4 feet, 2 putts inside 40 feet and 3 putts beyond that. Change these in Rules & clubs.

How do I play the same course as a friend? Share the course code from New round or the menu during a round. Your friend enters it under Enter a friend's code.

Do I need Game Center? No. It's off until you turn it on, and only adds leaderboards for the daily course and achievements.

How do I use Apple Watch? Start a round on your iPhone, then open Range Round on your watch. Your iPhone keeps the round, so it needs to be nearby.
