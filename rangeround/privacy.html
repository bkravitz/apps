Privacy policy

Range Round · Last updated November 5, 2026

Range Round does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and the app does not send data to any server run by its developer.

Data on your device

Your rounds, scores, club distances and settings are stored only on your iPhone. They are included in your device backups like other app data. Deleting the app deletes them.

Apple Watch

If you use the Apple Watch app, your round is passed directly between your iPhone and your watch. It is not sent anywhere else.

Game Center (optional)

Game Center is off unless you turn it on in the app. If you do, your daily-course scores and achievements are sent to Apple's Game Center service under your Game Center profile. Apple handles that information under Apple's privacy policy. You can turn Game Center off in the app at any time.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
